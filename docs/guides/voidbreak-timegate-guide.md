# The Voidbreak Timegate: How to Prepare and What to Do When It Lifts

A world-level timegate blocks the ascension from Incarnation into
Voidbreak until a fixed server day (roughly day 35–38 of a server's
life). This ascension is also the Mortal → Spiritual World boundary —
the fresh Myrimon tier and extractor ride on it. Handled well, the
gated weeks become a stockpile that carries you deep into Voidbreak the
day the gate opens. This piece covers what the gate changes: the
overcap mechanics, the Virya blessings, and gate-day routing. The general systems it leans on — daily pills, Creation
Artifacts, the extractor, the ascension-day checklist — are covered in
the calculator's Guide and Reference tabs and aren't repeated here. The
same pattern returns at every later realm gate.

## How the gate and Excess EXP work

- While the gate blocks your breakthrough, cultivation EXP keeps
  accruing past the full gauge into an **Excess EXP** pool, returned
  after the breakthrough. Nothing is lost by sitting at a full gauge.
- Breakthroughs are always **manual**. Stocked excess applies as you
  click through each grade — with a large pool you can clear entire
  half-steps in one go the moment you break through.
- The gauge percentage past 100% reads as: EXP gained since the start
  of your current half-step ÷ that half-step's total EXP. Overcap
  percentages therefore translate directly into future progress.

### What a given stock buys you (ascending from Incarnation Late)

| Half-step | Total EXP | Grades |
|---|---|---|
| Incarnation Late | 61.8M | — |
| Voidbreak Early | 68.0M | 20 |
| Voidbreak Middle | 142.1M | 20 |
| Voidbreak Late | 307.7M | 20 |

- **100%** — gauge full: take the Completion breakthrough (see below)
  and keep stocking.
- **210%** — the excess charges all of Voidbreak Early on ascension day.
- **440%** — the excess charges Early and Middle; you arrive at
  Voidbreak Late G1 immediately.

## Accrual rules while parked at a full gauge

While overcapped:

- You accrue at the **capped row's base band** — no future-row speed
  scaling.
- **Strive Bonus does not apply.** Server leaders lose nothing; the
  further behind the top player you are, the more parking
  under-performs your normal rate.
- **Virya blessing points apply in full.**
- Flat daily EXP — pills, Respira, elixirs, fruits — lands at face value
  into the pool, unaffected by parking.

Stage base bands rise with each half-step: Incarnation Late 0.40 →
Voidbreak Early 0.50 → Middle 0.65 → Late 0.80.

## Ascension Virya blessings: the biggest lever

Blessing points are the difference between a mediocre stock and a huge
one. Tiers unlock from your primary and secondary paths together:

- **Completion** — reach Incarnation Late 100% and **break through into
  Incarnation (Perfected)**. A full gauge alone is not enough: the
  blessing system does not start until this breakthrough is taken. It is
  not blocked by the timegate — the gate blocks only the ascension into
  Voidbreak — so take it the moment the gauge fills. It removes realm
  restrictions on cultivation pills (higher-rank pills can feed a lower
  secondary path) and unlocks pill auto-transmogrification, which is
  what makes the secondary rush below possible.
- **Perfection** — primary at Incarnation Completion, secondary at
  Nascent Soul Late, clear Amethyst Fiend in Myrimon Wonder:
  **+20 points** absorption.
- **Perfect** — secondary at Incarnation Middle, clear Jade-Eyed Lion:
  a further **+20 points** permanent, plus **+20 points active only
  before Voidbreak Middle**.

Secondary-path requirements are satisfied on **reaching** the named
half-step, not completing it. Fully built: +40 points permanent, +60
total until you enter Voidbreak Middle. On a 0.40 base band that is
more than double the parked passive rate.

The windowed +20 ends the instant you enter Voidbreak Middle — this
single fact drives the gate-day routing below.

## Preparing while gated

1. **Cap Incarnation early and take the Completion breakthrough at
   once.** Days spent climbing to the cap are not stocking days, and
   the gauge filling by itself starts nothing — the breakthrough into
   Incarnation (Perfected) is what opens the Virya system. Top off with
   banked fruits if the gauge won't fill on streams alone.
2. **Rush the Virya tiers immediately after the Completion
   breakthrough.** Divert your daily pills to the secondary path
   (passive stays on the primary): Nascent Soul Late unlocks the first
   +20, Incarnation Middle the rest. The earlier the tiers land, the
   longer they multiply your parked accrual. Clear the two Myrimon
   Wonder bosses ahead of time so they never hold a tier hostage.
3. **Fill every flat stream, every day.** Every pill, Respira attempt,
   and elixir lands in the pool at face value. Never leave pill
   attempts unused: pill EXP roughly halves per quality step, so a full
   limit of the next quality down matches a half-filled limit of the
   one above.
4. **Bank fruits while the extractor levels, and eat the bank before
   the gate opens.** Fruits lose their highest-rank +50% the moment the
   gate unlocks the next realm's fruit tier — eating a large bank after
   the gate wastes a third of it. Leftovers don't survive the ascension
   anyway: the mortal extractor resets at the World boundary and
   auto-consumes them at pre-upgrade rates.
5. **Hoard for the arrival — the parked weeks are the window.** Sect
   contribution (~13–14k) for the new realm's blueprints and formulas;
   Fateum and Fate Tokens, Revealstones, plant speed-ups; trove
   jadeslips for Cosmic Atlas, Ancient Treasure, and Pet Index — their
   contents re-tier on realm breakthrough, so opened on arrival they
   pay out at the new realm's tier. Don't run this hoard between gates:
   realm gates are months apart, and resources sat on for months are
   power you didn't use.
6. **Spend what dies with the realm.** Beyond the standard
   pre-breakthrough rules, spend Ability Knowledge and harvest the
   garden empty before ascending.
7. **Have breakthrough materials ready.** Excess EXP applies only as
   fast as you can click through breakthroughs; missing consumables are
   the only thing that can stall a charged climb.

## Gate day

1. **Ascend the moment the gate lifts.** Voidbreak Early's base band
   (0.50) beats Incarnation Late's (0.40) — whether you park or push,
   you accrue faster inside.
2. **Click through Voidbreak Early.** Your excess charges its grades
   instantly.
3. **Route by where the server's leaders are — not by your current
   Strive number.** Parked at the Early cap you accrue at (0.50 + 0.60
   blessing) with no Strive; live in Middle you accrue at
   (0.65 + 0.40) × (1 + Strive). Strive is measured against the
   server's top cultivator, so what matters is the Strive you would
   have **while in Middle**, not the one you see today:
   - **Never be the first into Middle.** While the leaders hold the
     Early cap, pushing past them makes you the front — your Strive
     drops away and you grind Middle at a flat 1.05, worse than
     parking at 1.10. Trailing players wait at the cap like everyone
     else.
   - **Front-runners:** stay parked until the pool covers all 142.1M
     of Middle, then clear it in one push and arrive at Voidbreak
     Late. A one-push spends no live time in Middle, so lost Strive
     never enters into it.
   - **After the leaders push to Late:** trailing players keep their
     Strive while climbing Middle live. Above ~5% Strive, pushing now
     beats staying parked (1.05 × (1 + Strive) > 1.10); below it, keep
     parking until your own pool covers the rest.

   Net effect: the server bunches at the Early cap, then peels off
   front to back.
4. **Move your streams up a tier.** Switch to the newly unlocked pill
   rank as soon as it's sustainable, start leveling the Spiritual
   World's fresh extractor with the new fruit income, open the saved
   jadeslips, and spend the hoarded sect contribution on the new
   realm's blueprints and formulas. The rest of arrival day — laws,
   Pandemonium, the treasure trove — is the standard ascension
   checklist in the calculator's Guide.

## What changes by account type

**Without the Vase (no Creation Artifacts).** Your pill stream is
exactly the daily limit, so quality per attempt is everything you
control there — and your prestock leans hardest on passive accrual,
which makes the Virya rush proportionally your biggest lever: +60
points on a 0.40–0.50 base band more than doubles the parked rate.
Fruits are your swing resource; bank them well.

**With the Vase (and Mirror).** Refined red pills bypass the daily
limit, so a fed Vase adds stock at face value every single parked day,
and the Mirror stacks copies on top. Keep them fed for the whole gated
stretch — artifact energy sitting at its cap is stock lost.

**Free-to-play.** Fruits are the main F2P tool for meeting timegates,
and blessings are progression-gated, not paid — a built secondary path
is worth more than any consumable. Sustain the best pill quality you
can, but a full limit of a lower quality still beats a half-filled
limit of a higher one.

**Paying.** The two standout paid levers during a gate: the daily
artifact charges (the cheapest EXP money buys) and the three elixir
packs offered on entering the new realm — take those at Voidbreak, not
before, and see the calculator's spending guide for the full priority
list. Sustained higher pill quality widens the stock linearly.

**Underdeveloped secondary path.** The blessing tiers need the
secondary at Nascent Soul Late, then Incarnation Middle. Completion's
realm-restriction removal exists exactly to fix this: the moment it
lands, divert your now-unrestricted daily pills to the secondary and
power-level it. Until the tiers land, you park at base band —
well under half the blessed rate — so every day of delay is expensive.

## The one rule that generalizes

At every realm gate: cap early, take the breakthrough that opens the
blessings, boost absorption before parking, stock every flat stream
into the pool, and never enter a half-step that carries a windowed
penalty for you until you can clear it whole. The calculator's
prestock mode models all of the above — enter your gate date to see
where your stock will land you.
