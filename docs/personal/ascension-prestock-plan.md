timegate lifting 2026-08-09 (24 days from day 0 = Wed 2026-07-16).

play. Ascend the moment the gate lifts anyway** (inside Voidbreak Early the

1. Days 0–3: passive+pills stay on primary; reach the Incarnation cap

5. Eat weekly fruits on receipt (always primary); eat the held bank when
   the extractor caps (~day 12). Eat-now vs hold was computed as a wash

6. Every week: hold ranking 2–5 and buy the Monday pack — worth ~45 gate
   points combined, more than every other micro-decision.
7. 2026-08-09: ascend immediately at ~379% stocked; the remaining ~60M
   closes ~5 days inside Voidbreak.
